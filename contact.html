Before you write

Four answers, already written down.

Most messages fall into one of these four buckets, and the fastest answer is usually the one we have already published.

Give a monthly gift
Receipts go out by email within 24 hours of a successful gift, then again in January with your full giving summary. If nothing has arrived after ten business days, email giving@hearthfoundation.org with the gift date and we resend within one business day.
Regional coordinators review applications every Tuesday and Thursday. You will get either an interview invitation or a placement suggestion within seven days. Field placements in remote regions can take up to four weeks because of safeguarding and travel checks.
Yes. We issue donor-advised fund grants, employee-match reimbursements, and a single invoice for multi-team giving. Send your requirements and finance contact to partnerships@hearthfoundation.org and we will send a capability statement and W-9 within three business days.
We accept unopened medical supplies, water filters and school materials at our regional depots, Monday to Friday, 9am to 4pm. We cannot take food, clothing or second-hand furniture because of handling and storage costs. Email the depot first so we can confirm storage space.
Write to us

Send the detail once. We will not ask again.

Tell us what you need and how to reach you. The form routes itself to the right regional team, so nobody forwards your message twice.

  • Replies within one business day, Monday to Friday
  • Urgent safeguarding issues are triaged the same day
  • We never share your details outside the team you contact
  • Accessible-format requests are always welcome

Safeguarding reports: please use the confidential hotline rather than this form, so nothing sits in an unattended inbox.
