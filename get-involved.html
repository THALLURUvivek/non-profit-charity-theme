What your time does

Volunteers are not free labour. They are the margin that makes it work.

Local crew leads are paid. Volunteers fill the surge — the extra hands during a two-week build, the interpreter on a monitoring visit, the mentor who stays after the funding ends.

Apply

Tell us what you are actually good at.

We read every application ourselves and reply within ten working days — including when the answer is no.

  • No unpaid labour placements

    If the work needs doing daily and indefinitely, we will pay you for it or fund a local to do it.

  • Safeguarding first

    All placements touching children require an enhanced background check, renewed every two years.

  • Flights covered

    For two-week builds we pay flights, lodging and visas. Remote roles are always unpaid — we say so up front.

Practicalities

Before you apply.

For field builds of two weeks or more, yes — flights, lodging, visas and vaccinations. For shorter monitoring trips we cover a per-diem. Remote roles are never paid, and we will tell you that before you apply, not after.
Eighteen for unsupervised field placements, sixteen when accompanied by a parent or guardian. Remote mentoring roles are open from sixteen with a guardian on the account.
On family build weeks in the dry season, yes — for ages eight and up there is supervised school-support work to do. Tell us their ages in the application and we will confirm suitability.
A named coordinator reviews it within ten working days. If there is a match we set up a 30-minute call. If there is not, we will say so and suggest a role that fits better — usually something more remote.
Tell us anyway. Dental hygiene, GIS, structural engineering, sign language, bookkeeping in French — a third of our best-matched volunteers applied to roles that did not exist when they wrote to us.
Only with a signed, informed, revocable consent form — separately for each use. You can withdraw consent afterwards and we remove the material, though we cannot recall what has already been reprinted.
