How we work

Four promises we can be held to.

Accountability is not a brochure page for us. It is the product. Here is exactly what we commit to â€” and how you can verify each one.

Radical transparency

Bank statements, executive pay and every sub-grant above $1,000 are published unedited each quarter. Three independent audits a year.

Local leadership

73% of our programme budget flows to organisations headquartered in the countries we serve. Our Seattle office runs on 6%.

Long horizons

We commit to ten-year cycles. A borehole drilled in year one is still delivering in year ten â€” that is how we measure success.

Donors stay in charge

Change or cancel a monthly gift in one email, with no phone call and no retention offer. Restricted gifts are never moved without your written consent.

The Hearth method

Four steps, ten years, no shortcuts.

Our process is deliberately slow at the start. That is what makes the last nine years hold.

01

We listen first

Six months of house calls and community assemblies before a single budget line is written.

02

Local boards lead

A community board of nine sets priorities, signs the budget and holds the implementation contract.

03

We build together

Paid local crews, not flown-in contractors. Skills transfer on day one, not at handover.

04

We hand it over

Ownership, land title and the maintenance fund transfer to the board. We stay as auditors only.
