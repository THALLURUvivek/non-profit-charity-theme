Deep dive · Clean water

The most generous gift is the one that still works in year five.

Across our network, 31% of hand pumps worldwide break within two years. Almost always for the same reason: nobody had money set aside for the spare part. We changed the budget, not the pump.

18% of budget

Reserved for maintenance before the first shovel goes in.

Nine seats

Every committee has a rotating women-only majority.

94% uptime

Measured across 486 sites by SMS, twice a month.

$0.03 / litre

Affordability capped at 3% of household income.

Deep dive · Education

A scholarship is a bridge. A school is the road.

Stipends get girls into the classroom. What keeps them there is teaching quality, sanitary facilities and a reason to believe the certificate is worth something. We fund all four.

  • Termly coaching, not workshops

    Teachers get in-classroom coaching every fortnight, tracked against a structured lesson plan.

  • Sanitation is not optional

    Every school in the network has private, lockable, ventilated facilities for girls.

  • We measure comprehension, not attendance

    Termly oral comprehension checks are the only attendance-independent metric we report to the board.

+34%

rise in reading comprehension after two terms

0.4%

of the national health budget this work replaces

Deep dive · Climate & coast

A metre of mangrove buys a village a decade.

Sea walls made of concrete are the standard answer and the standard failure. Mangrove belts cost a fifth as much, hold carbon, feed fishing households and repair themselves — provided the village trust that manages them survives long enough to see the payoff.

9,400 ha restored

Replanted with 380 village trusts holding the seawall contracts.

640 seed varieties

Local drought-tolerant lines held in community seed banks.

380 villages warned

Community-run early-warning beacons, 41 minutes median lead time.

21% less damage

Losses in restored districts vs. the 2019 cyclone baseline.
